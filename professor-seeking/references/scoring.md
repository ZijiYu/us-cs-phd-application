# Scoring and ranking

Score each candidate who survived the coarse filter. The numbers are a way to make trade-offs explicit, not a substitute for judgment: when you override the score, say why in one line.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| **Fit** | 0–5 | 5: papers in the last two years on the applicant's core problem, with a method or evaluation the applicant's project could plausibly extend. 4: same problem, different method or modality. 3: adjacent area with a concrete bridge (shared dataset, failure mode, evaluation gap). 2: same broad area, bridge is generic. 1: overlap only in keywords. 0: no real overlap. |
| **Recruiting** | 0–3 | 3: official page or the professor's own post says recruiting for the target term. 2: general "always looking for students", or a post from the last 6 months. 1: nothing public. 0: says not recruiting (exclude). |
| **Stage** | 0–2 | 2: assistant professor who started within ~2 years (building a first cohort). 1: assistant professor 3–6 years in. 0: tenured / senior. |
| **Competitiveness adjustment** | −2 to +1 | Depends on the cycle tier (table below). |

**Total = 2 × Fit + Recruiting + Stage + adjustment.** Fit is doubled because a weak fit makes every email generic, and no recruiting signal can rescue that.

### Competitiveness adjustment by cycle tier

| Competitiveness | Safety (保底) | Target | Reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | +1 | 0 | 0 |
| Medium | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| High | −2 | −1 | 0 |

## Competitiveness proxies

Rate Low / Medium / High from whatever is available; two or three proxies are enough.

- **Seniority**: endowed chair, named professor, fellow → leans High; new assistant professor → leans Low.
- **Group size**: many current PhD students and postdocs → High demand, but also more slots; a lab page listing 0–3 students → Low.
- **Visibility**: high citations or h-index relative to career length, best-paper awards, large industry-adjacent projects, frequent press.
- **Star group**: part of a well-known lab with several prominent PIs (e.g. a school's flagship NLP or vision group) → High.
- **Output rate**: very high recent top-venue counts usually mean a large, sought-after group.

## Hard excludes

Left the school, emeritus, explicitly not recruiting for the term, already contacted this cycle (unless a follow-up is planned), or ruled out by the user. List them in the output's excluded section with the reason.

## Tie-breaks

More recent relevant first-author or last-author work; a stated preference for the applicant's background (for example "students with HCI or industry experience welcome"); evidence the professor replies to prospective students (alumni reports, posts inviting emails).
